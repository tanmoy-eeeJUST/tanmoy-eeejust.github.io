Research

My work sits between signal processing and machine learning for health. I care about models that are accurate and also explain why they decided what they decided.

  • Biomedical signal processing. sEMG and EEG denoising, decomposition and feature extraction.
  • Explainable machine learning. Making classification results interpretable for clinical use.
  • Metaheuristic feature selection. Finding small, informative feature sets.
  • Deep learning. Sequence and transfer-learning models for medical data.

Current M.Sc. thesis: an LLM-based explainable framework for stroke prediction using EEG and EMG biosignals.
